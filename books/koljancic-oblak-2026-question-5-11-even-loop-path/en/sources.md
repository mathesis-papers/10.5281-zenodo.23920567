Oblak, *Combinatorial aspects of the nonsymmetric strong spectral property for graphs*, Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra **42** (2026), 755–779, [DOI:10.13001/ela.2026.10523](https://doi.org/10.13001/ela.2026.10523); [arXiv:2604.21552v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21552v1). Theorem 5.7(Cc) gives the odd-order obstruction, and Theorem 5.7(A) supplies the interior-loop failure construction.
- Carptopus, *Spectral separation and the non-symmetric strong spectral property for looped double paths*, dated 19 August 2026, revised 22 August 2026; [dated source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Carptopus/MathExplore-Notes/e57ee007380cbc351eb371d8e991303cab5f1e48/research/nssp-looped-double-paths/nssp-looped-double-paths.tex), version 0.2 [DOI:10.5281/zenodo.22059007](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22059007). The present manuscript acknowledges this priority and uses the same parity separation mechanism.
- J. Michor and G. Teschl, *Reconstructing Jacobi matrices from three spectra*, in *Spectral Methods for Operators of Mathematical Physics*, OTAA **154** (2004), 151–154, [DOI:10.1007/978-3-0348-7947-7_9](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-0348-7947-7_9). ## What the formal statements cover

The [even-order Lean theorem](lean:D5/S3/Combinatorics/SpectralGraph/KoljancicOblakEvenPathNSSP.result) states existence of a real witness with exact support for every positive even order and nonempty loop set, with an unrestricted real test matrix. The [branch-recovery theorem](lean:D5/S3/Combinatorics/SpectralGraph/KoljancicOblakEvenPath.branch_variation_injective) states the all-length recovery lemma. Those are the two exact correspondences displayed in [the statement chapter](statements.md).

The broader written arguments establish symmetry and positive integrality, the odd-order criterion, joined recovery without restricting the parity of $r+s$, integer branch separation, the fixed-matrix spectral equivalence, the gcd test, and the family consequences. The source reports finite rational checks through order eight for all loop sets, through ten for branch differentials, through twelve for the 78 single-loop positions, and orders three through twelve for the interior failure construction. These are reported finite checks; the all-orders conclusions follow from the written arguments.

## Focused contributions

The shared requests coordinate formalization of proved mathematics:

- [General joined recovery](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/154): reuse the existing branch recovery and characteristic-polynomial differential, and remove the extra parity hypothesis on $r+s$.
- [Integer branch separation](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/155): formalize the explicit $M=d+1$ construction for every admissible branch parity.
- [Full existence criterion](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/156): use those prerequisites for odd order and positive-integer symmetric witnesses; retain the existing exact even-order theorem.
- [Single-loop equivalence and gcd test](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/157): reuse the recovery argument, add the common-eigenvalue obstruction, then deduce the arithmetic criterion.
- [Loop families and superpatterns](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing-requests/issues/158): deduce the classifications from the criterion and spectral equivalence, preserving the small-order cases and explicit failure matrices.

Coordinate scope on the canonical request, then use a focused branch in a contributor's fork and a pull request directly to [upstream dev](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/blob/dev/docs/CONTRIBUTING.md) under its existing rules. The original problem authors, earlier proof author, manuscript authors and formalization contributors retain their respective credit. A merged pull request and a platform verification receipt are distinct evidence.
